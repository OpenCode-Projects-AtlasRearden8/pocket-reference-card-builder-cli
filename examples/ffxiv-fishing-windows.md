# FFXIV Fishing Windows

Mechanics reminders for discerning anglers - how windows work, not a data dump. Always re-verify bite timings in-game or on Teamcraft for the current patch.

---

# How a Window Works

- Time window: bite chance is elevated only during certain Earth-time hours for that fish
- Weather window: many fish bite only during, or immediately after, a specific weather
- Transition bonus: some conditionals bite best the day after a weather change
- Fisher's Intuition: certain fish unlock only after catching another fish first - chain the trigger catch, then the target
- Spot rules override: if the log marks a fish as any-time, ignore old notes that say otherwise

---

# The Eorzean Clock

- Time math: one Eorzean day is 70 Earth minutes, so a 9-to-12 window runs about 9 Earth minutes
- Each clock chime is one Eorzean minute - pace windows by chimes, never by wall time
- Arrive early: be in position, baited, with GP topped up before the window opens
- Log resets: fishing logs refresh hourly - position yourself ahead of the hour mark
- Log progress: any size catch counts for the log - HQ is for fish markets, not completion

---

# Bait, Tug, and GP Discipline

- Small fry first: cheap bites still eat the window - dismiss junk efficiently
- Tug types: light, medium, heavy - Patience users must hook with the matching Precision/Powerful Hooking
- Snagging: keep it off unless the log flags item-required catches, or you are on gear-less ledges
- GP habits: bank 400 GP before the window opens - budget for Cast and Patience, not panic
- Efficiency: leaving mid-window wastes the rare weather - waiting usually costs less

---

# Mooching Chains

- Mooch rule: your catch can be re-cast as live bait immediately
- Chain order: catch the trigger fish, then mooch in the same water type
- Mooch II: double-mooch fish exist - expect a second stage and a shorter effective window
- Do not release: hours of a chain lost to one wrong discard - keep the fish you need
- Bait fridge: stock the water you need for both stages before the window starts

---

# Big Fish Habits

- Patience tiers: both Patience skills change tug priority - only use them when the log entry agrees
- Large fish with smaller bite windows: treat every medium tug as a keeper until the flag says otherwise
- Failure mode: leaving to fish elsewhere mid-window - sit it out instead
- Finicky lunkers: bank attempts, not technique - patience plus presence wins

---

# Log-Completion Routine

- One card row per fish: spot, bait, time chimes, weather, trigger fish
- Restock bait before the trip, never mid-window
- Unverified bite time = park it as a note for next trip instead of guessing
