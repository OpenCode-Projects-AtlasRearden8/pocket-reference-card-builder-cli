# KDP Deadline Rules

Checklist-style rules of thumb for publishing on Kindle Direct Publishing. Rules change often - confirm every item against the current KDP help pages before you commit money or a launch date.

---

# File and Metadata Basics

- Manuscript: DOC/DOCX, KPF via Kindle Create, EPUB, or PDF (print only)
- Cover: eBook needs a separate cover image; print interiors wrap spine+back+front in one PDF
- Previewer before publish: run the Kindle Previewer on device-type states - phone, tablet, e-reader
- Specs: 72x72 cover minimum is weak - use 1600x2560 or better
- Metadata prep: title page should not repeat the author name - the metadata field does that

---

# Review and Go-Live Timing

- Review queues: most books clear automated review in hours; plan for up to 72 - not guaranteed
- Live vs buyable: book appears at its launch time only after review passes
- Promo pricing changes: price drops can lag - set sale dates well ahead of the sale window
- Update cycle: re-published interior files re-enter review - schedule edits early
- Buffer rule of thumb: launch date includes a 5-7 day review window and a 3-4 day soft landing

---

# KDP Select and Promotions

- Enroll per 90-day term: Select exclusivity buys you the promo toolkit
- Free days: up to 5 per 90-day term for enrolled eBooks - limited in total
- Countdown Deals: discount keeps a 70% royalty tier during the promo window
- Category and keyword updates: allowed as needed - refresh quarterly or on trend shift
- eBook royalty tiers: 35% or 70% - watch delivery cost and price bands before you set price

---

# Pricing and Royalty Quick Math

- 70% tier range: roughly $2.99-$9.99 for most eBooks with a delivery fee per MB
- 35% tier: everything else - forced when out-of-band pricing or exclusive regions apply
- Print cost formula: unit cost drives the minimum list price - room for a royalty only above it
- Hardback path: separate edition, separate review, separate pricing floor
- Coupon discipline: never launch a promo the same week as a file update

---

# Launch-Week Checklist

- T-21 days: manuscript frozen, cover settled, pre-order opened
- T-14 days: categories locked in (any 3-category choice confirmed)
- T-7 days: review queue verified, price schedule pinned
- T-0: newsletter to list, launch posts ready, links all live
- T+7: read reviews, A/B the price, refresh keywords
